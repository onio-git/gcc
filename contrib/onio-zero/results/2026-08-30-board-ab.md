Test the release artifacts directly; do not relink or otherwise rebuild them.
Verify each SHA-256 before flashing:

All three images use `TOTAL_DATA_SIZE=2000`, `ITERATIONS=1`, and
`CPU_FREQ_HZ=32000000`.  The one-iteration harness prints the expected
ten-second-duration warning.  A valid run must report:

```text
seedcrc      0xe9f5
crclist      0xe714
crcmatrix    0x1fd7
crcstate     0x8e3a
crcfinal     0xe714
```

The target-default versus unpadded comparison measures the complete selective
LTO, dead-section removal, and function-layout candidate.  The unpadded versus
16-byte comparison isolates the fixed-image cache phase: the two images have
the same timed instruction and branch counts in the model and differ by the
16-byte startup/text pad.

Alternate all three exact images on the same Aldebaran board under the same
conditions.  Keep every individual result rather than only an average.  The
test report must include:

- the observed SHA-256 and CRC output for every image;
- board and silicon revision, clock, supply voltage, flash configuration, and
  temperature or ambient conditions;
- flash/debug tool and command line;
- run order and individual runtime or cycle measurement;
- raw power trace and per-run energy when power is measured; and
- any resets, warm-up runs, discarded runs, or failures.

The modeled numbers and complete build provenance are in
`2026-08-26-function-layout.md`.  Hardware results must be reported separately;
the simulator result is not a hardware performance or energy claim.
